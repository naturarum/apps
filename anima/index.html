Anima

A physical-modeling synthesizer for iPad. No oscillators and no samples: a breath excites a resonating object, and it rings out into a room. Its heart is a modal resonator, so every note lands exactly in tune. Anima is the companion to Hexatone.

What it is

The instrument

Energy → object → room

No oscillators and no samples. A breath excites a resonating object, and the object rings out into a room. That is the whole interface, read left to right.

The object

A modal resonator

Twenty-four coupled resonances per voice, computed from the note. Make it wood, glass, or metal, and stretch its partials from strings into bells. Push Inertia far enough and the note ignites and sings on its own breath.

Tuning

Every note in tune

Because pitch is computed, every note is exactly in tune, including any microtonal pitch an MPE controller sends. Route Hexatone's MPE in and its per-note microtuning just works, with nothing to configure.

The look

A panel that comes alive

Every control is a glyph whose shape is its value, so the panel is a picture of the current sound. Touch one and it moves; load a preset and the whole surface morphs. Dressed in a warm dusk palette of sand, ochre, and sage.

The room

A room you can morph

One space, from a tight slap to an endless wash. Size sets the echo, Regen runs all the way into self-oscillation, and Blur melts the repeats into reverb.

Play it

Ribbon, keys, and MPE

Play on a continuous-pitch ribbon or a three-octave piano, from a single voice up to sixty-four. Standalone or as an AUv3 in AUM, Logic, GarageBand, and Cubasis, with MPE expression and a sustain pedal.

In short

A companion instrument, in the same family

Anima shares Hexatone's microtonal heart but stands on its own: a deep physical-modeling synth for iPad, dressed in a warm dusk palette of sand, clay, ochre, and sage. Breathy reeds, struck bells and metal, and tones no acoustic instrument could make. Factory presets arrive in banks, including an artist pack, and you can save your own or copy any sound as text to send to a friend. One price, no in-app purchases, no subscription.
