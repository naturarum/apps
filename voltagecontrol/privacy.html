VoltageControl Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

VoltageControl does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app runs entirely on your device. It sends MIDI to other apps and hardware you choose to connect it to, and it saves your presets and settings locally on your device. None of this leaves your device, and none of it is sent to the developer or to any third party.

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No network requests.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

The app uses on-device storage (for your presets and settings) only.
